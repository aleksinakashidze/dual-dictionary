# Account & Data Deletion — Dual Dictionary

**Last updated: 4 July 2026**

This page explains how to request deletion of your **Dual Dictionary** account and the data associated with it.

Developer: **Dual Dictionary** · Contact: **info.dualdictionary.team@gmail.com**

---

## How to request account deletion

You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time:

1. From the email address you used to register in the Dual Dictionary app, send an email to **info.dualdictionary.team@gmail.com**.
2. Use the subject line: **Delete my account**.
3. In the message, confirm the email address linked to your Dual Dictionary account.

We will verify the request and delete your account. Requests are normally processed within **30 days**.

---

## What data is deleted

When your account is deleted, we permanently remove:

- Your **account information** (email address and name)
- Your **study list** (saved words)
- Your **quiz** activity and progress
- Any **PDF files** you uploaded and the **highlights** you created

---

## What data is kept

- We do **not** retain your personal account data after deletion is completed.
- Limited, non-identifying **server logs** may be retained for a short period for security and legal compliance, after which they are automatically removed.

---

## Questions

If you have any questions about account or data deletion, contact us at **info.dualdictionary.team@gmail.com**.
