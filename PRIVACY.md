# Privacy Policy — Dual Dictionary

**Last updated: 4 July 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how the **Dual Dictionary** mobile application ("the App", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects your information. By using the App you agree to the practices described here.

If you have any questions, contact us at **info.dualdictionary.team@gmail.com**.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information needed to provide the App's features.

### a. Account information
When you register or sign in, we collect:
- Your **email address**
- Your **name** (if provided or supplied by Google Sign-In)
- A securely hashed **password** (for email/password accounts)

If you sign in with **Google**, we receive your basic Google profile information (email address and name) through Google Sign-In. We do **not** receive or store your Google password.

### b. Content you create in the App
- Words you save to your **study list**
- Your **quiz** activity and progress
- **PDF files** you upload to the in-app library and the **highlights** you create in them

### c. Technical information
- **Authentication tokens**, stored securely on your device (via the operating system's secure storage) and never shared with third parties.
- Basic **server logs** (such as request timestamps and error information) generated when the App communicates with our backend, used for security and troubleshooting.

We do **not** collect your precise location, contacts, photos, or advertising identifiers, and we do **not** use third-party advertising or analytics tracking.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:
- Create and secure your account and sign you in
- Provide dictionary search, study lists, quizzes, and the PDF library
- Save and sync your personal content across sessions
- Maintain the security, reliability, and performance of the service
- Respond to your support requests

We do **not sell** your personal information to anyone.

---

## 3. How We Share Information

We share information only as necessary to operate the App:
- **Google** — when you choose Google Sign-In, authentication is handled by Google under [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **Hosting provider** — our backend and data are hosted on cloud infrastructure (Render) solely to run the service.

We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights, safety, and security of our users and service.

---

## 4. Data Storage and Security

- Your data is transmitted over encrypted **HTTPS** connections.
- Passwords are stored only in **hashed** form.
- Authentication tokens are kept in your device's **secure storage**, not in general app storage.

While we take reasonable measures to protect your data, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

---

## 5. Data Retention and Deletion

We keep your information for as long as your account is active.

You may request **deletion of your account and associated data** at any time by emailing **info.dualdictionary.team@gmail.com**. We will delete your personal data within a reasonable period, except where we are required to retain it by law.

---

## 6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

---
